## Results in brief (aa44c1d9)

- Locked gate 2a on aa44c1d9: passed, every step (observations, ratchet, compare, l0, coverage, runners, l1, seeded, inventory, main) and checks 1, 2, 7, 10 and 12, judged by the checkpoint f133c4b8's harness in ref-pandas3; it also passed on f133c4b8 itself, judged by 0b6d9a12's.
- Census: 41 sites, each with a probe (`census: ok`). The core refuses where the reference answers at one site, on DuckDB only: rule `aggregation`, a LIST_DISTINCT of a foreign key, kept until Phase 4 (P2E-19, KO2-75). On SQLite and Postgres, none.
- Kept rules on real data: `text_time_rules` 0 and `model` 0 (KO2-90, the owner's), `aggregation` 0 (KO2-92, the orchestrator's under KO2-88), on all three databases.
- Coverage `--check`: ok. The S1 listing: 17 of 75 S0 and S1 paths and 22 of 44 in-scope cells have an acceptance case.
- H24 (be55838a's CI run): `compare: no differences`; 16,054 non-gating diagnostics: 9,396 `sql.text` and 6,623 `sql.parameters` (the core's Postgres SQL is its own), and 35 others ([h24/other-diagnostics.txt](h24/other-diagnostics.txt): registered outcome differences on `s0.boundary.*` cases and statement counts of registered early refusals).
- L3: generated, 200 sources: 166 agree, 26 under P1B-11, 8 classified by hand (7 under P2B-1, 1 under DT-15). Selfref, 9 sources: 4 agree, 5 under P1B-13. None unclassified.
- L7: median core-to-reference CPU 0.582 over 175 scenarios, none above 1.1; import time 1,008 against 1,078 ms; native library 1.170 times 91a00319's (limit 1.25); boundary overhead, message sizes and no pandas in providers hold. The 4 statement-count lines are registered refusals that stop before materialize's reads.
- Unseeded sampling: 9 of 9 pass on DuckDB, SQLite and Postgres.
- Held-out run (a2178e5b): 47 cases, 140 legs (DuckDB 47, SQLite 46, Postgres 47). Panics 0; new refusals with no approval 0. python-core: no leg differs from the reference. rust-host: 4 cases (12 legs) differ, each where the scripted endpoint refuses a call (the reference raises NimRequestError, rust-host ends INVALID_RESPONSE); P2G-1 covers 2 of them by name, so 2 count as unapproved (`h2.calls.only-call-fails`, `h2.rank.second-candidate-call-fails`). Within H11's threshold (more than 4 unapproved cases is a no-go): GO.
- Flip dry run: unforced 2,789 passed; forced 118 failed of 2,789, all in three files: `test_the_time_column_is_inferred_alike.py` (61), `test_a_key_is_counted_where_its_rows_are.py` (38), `test_tables_stay_where_they_are.py` (19).
